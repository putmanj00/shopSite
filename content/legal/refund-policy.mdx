# Artisan Guarantee & Returns

*Last updated: February 27, 2026*

We stand behind every piece we make. If something isn't right, we want to make it right.

## The 14-Day Window

You have 14 days from the date your order arrives to request a return. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition.

## One-of-a-Kind Disclaimer

Many Wildenflower pieces are truly one of a kind. Slight variations in dye pattern, color intensity, and texture between the photographed item and your item are natural — and they are not defects. They are what make your treasure yours.

## What Can't Be Returned

- Custom or personalized orders
- Final Sale items (marked clearly at time of purchase)

## Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect. If we made a mistake, we'll make it right at no cost to you.

## How to Start a Return

Email us at [wildenflowercreations@gmail.com](mailto:wildenflowercreations@gmail.com) with your order number and a brief description of the issue. We'll respond within 2 business days.

## Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receiving your returned item. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
