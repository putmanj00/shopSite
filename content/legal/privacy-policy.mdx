# Your Privacy is Sacred

*Last updated: February 27, 2026*

When you shop at Wildenflower, your privacy is treated with the same care we pour into every handcrafted piece. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and who ever sees it.

## What We Collect

We collect only what's needed to complete your order: your name, shipping address, and email address. We never store credit card numbers — payment processing is handled securely by Shopify.

## Your Email & the Trippy Tribe

If you subscribe to our newsletter, you're joining the Trippy Tribe — our little community of people who love handmade, botanical, and one-of-a-kind things. This is always opt-in. You can unsubscribe any time with one click, no questions asked.

## Who Sees Your Data

Your information is shared only with the essential service providers who make your order happen: Shopify (storefront & checkout), Resend (order confirmation emails), and your shipping carrier. We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising purposes.

## Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart intact and to understand how people find us (analytics). A cookie consent banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies.

## Your Rights

You have the right to request, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Email us at [wildenflowercreations@gmail.com](mailto:wildenflowercreations@gmail.com) and we'll take care of it.

## Contact

Questions? Reach us at [wildenflowercreations@gmail.com](mailto:wildenflowercreations@gmail.com).
